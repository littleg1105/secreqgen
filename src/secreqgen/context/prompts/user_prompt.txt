## Task
Write the system context of this product. Use only what the parts above say.

- name: the name of the product.
- description: one sentence, 25 words at most. It says what the product does and for whom.
- tech_stack: the main technologies of the product: language, framework, database, cache, search, message queue, frontend. Give a technology only if a part names it.
- security_profile: how much harm a security failure of this product can do: "Critical", "High", "Medium" or "Low". Then " - " and a short reason: what the product keeps and who uses it, as the parts show it. This is your judgement; the person will check it.
- features: 3 to 6 groups of features. Each group has a short name and 2 to 6 short features, 8 words at most each.
- data_sensitivity: the kinds of data that the product keeps, in three lists. critical: for example passwords, keys and tokens. high: private data of users. medium: other data of the product.

Each item names its source: the source name of the part that says it, for example "F3". Do not write an item that no part supports.
When the parts do not say something, leave it out. An empty list is a correct answer.
