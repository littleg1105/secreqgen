Task: choose a threat actor and a STRIDE category for each threat.

User story: $story_title. $story_text

Threats (each has a number):
$threats

For each threat choose two values.

The threat actor: who would most likely cause the threat. One value of this list:
- external_individual: one person outside the organisation that runs the system
- external_group: persons outside the organisation who work together, for example a criminal group
- external_organization: a different organisation with a connection to the system, for example a partner, a supplier or a competitor
- nation_state: a government or an agency of a state
- trusted_insider: a person with ordinary access, for example an employee or a member
- privileged_insider: a person with administrator or operator rights
- accidental_actor: a user or an administrator who causes harm by mistake
- other: none of these fits

The STRIDE category: what the threat does to security. One letter of this list:
- S (spoofing): somebody uses a false identity
- T (tampering): data or code is changed with no permission
- R (repudiation): somebody can deny an action, because there is no record of it
- I (information disclosure): data is shown to somebody who must not see it
- D (denial of service): the service stops or becomes too slow
- E (elevation of privilege): somebody gets rights or access that they must not have
- none: the sentence is no security threat, for example a feature that does not work or a plain fact about the product

Answer with one line for each threat and nothing else: the number, a colon, the actor, a comma and the STRIDE value. Example: 1: external_individual, S
