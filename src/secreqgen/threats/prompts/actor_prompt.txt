Task: choose a threat actor for each threat.

User story: $story_title. $story_text

Threats (each has a number):
$threats

For each threat choose the threat actor that fits best: who would most likely cause it. Use one value of this list:
- external_individual: one person outside the organisation that runs the system
- external_group: persons outside the organisation who work together, for example a criminal group
- external_organization: a different organisation with a connection to the system, for example a partner, a supplier or a competitor
- nation_state: a government or an agency of a state
- trusted_insider: a person with ordinary access, for example an employee or a member
- privileged_insider: a person with administrator or operator rights
- accidental_actor: a user or an administrator who causes harm by mistake
- other: none of these fits

Answer with one line for each threat and nothing else: the number, a colon and the value. Example: 1: external_individual
