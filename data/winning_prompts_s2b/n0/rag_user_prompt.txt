## Few-shot exemplars (anchor format, depth, ASVS mapping)

The following two examples show the level of specificity expected. The exemplar tokens (AUTH_PROVIDER_ATTRIBUTE_KEY, WORKER_UPLOAD_BACKEND, /api/v2/session/revoke) are FICTITIOUS — they are deliberately drawn from a hypothetical project and are not from any real codebase. Do not copy their content; use them to calibrate the form.
